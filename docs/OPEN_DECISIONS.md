# Open decisions

These decisions require owner approval before production accounts or legal terms
are created.

## Licensing

Select a software licence and a separate licence for original editorial content.
Possible software approaches include a permissive licence or AGPL if hosted
derivatives should remain open. Third-party and official source material retains
its own licensing conditions.

## Cloud accounts and regions

- Supabase organisation, plan, and UK/EU region
- Vercel organisation and project
- Production domain
- Cloud email provider for notifications
- Monitoring and incident-alert destination

## Editorial governance

- Who can approve an update for publication?
- Which changes require two-person review?
- How are reviewer identities represented publicly?
- What correction and user-notification service levels are promised?

## Case-law ingestion permissions

Confirm the intended use with The National Archives and obtain any permission
required before performing activity classified as computational analysis or bulk
reuse of Find Case Law material.

## Accounts and privacy

Decide whether the first release needs accounts. The recommended MVP works without
one; cloud-synchronised bookmarks and notifications can be introduced later with a
clear privacy notice, deletion workflow, and retention schedule.
